Project & Method

KUVHURA: Project & Model Guide

KUVHURA is a political-economy observatory for examining how resource control, patronage, coercion, livelihood options, and civic agency interact. The dashboard combines scenario tools, public-source indicators, audit material, regional comparisons, and submitted reports; these inputs do not all have the same evidential status.

Regime Durability Index (Φ)

The index compares elite resource and enforcement capacity with citizen livelihood independence and diaspora support. The implementation uses a small stabilizer to avoid division by zero:

Φ = (E × Pr × CL) / (AL × DB + 0.1)

Model interpretation: Φ > 60 = captive-hegemony scenario; 30–60 = contested attrition; Φ < 30 = pre-rupture fragility. These are heuristic scenario bands, not calibrated forecasts.

Realized Praxis Yield

This ratio represents reflection and action relative to structural and social friction:

Yield = (R × A) / (P + I + C)

The simulator derives proxy values for R, A, P, I, and C from its controls. The survey importer averages groups of four 1–5 Likert responses and multiplies each average by 2 to standardize it to 1–10 before calculating yield. The yield is not a direct observation of individual behavior.

Evidence & Live Feeds

The hashtag panel combines recent public Bluesky search results with public Mastodon #Zimbabwe timelines when those feeds are available, then compares hashtag mentions per post in the newest and preceding halves of the sample. It is not a measure of all social media, sentiment, polarization, or private messaging. Other source badges indicate an integration or local dataset, not necessarily a continuously updated official feed.

Regional Model & Risk Signals

Country profiles use regional Φ = (E × Pr × CL) / (AL × DB + 0.35) and a separate Praxis proxy, (8 × AL) / (E + Pr + CL + 0.2); these differ from the main simulator equations. Values without a verified live source are scenario estimates. The hotspot score sums each submitted amount plus its category weight by locality. Reports remain unverified until reviewed; these are screening heuristics, not causal findings or event forecasts.

Biography & Research Lens

About Privilege Kandira

Combining lived experience in civic leadership with formal academic rigor in international relations, sustainable development, and data-driven decision-making.

I am a political economy researcher and quantitative analyst specializing in governance dynamics, state captivity, and civic behavior in Southern Africa. My academic foundation spans a Master of Science in International Relations and a Bachelor of Science in International Environment and Development Studies, reinforced by ongoing vocational specialization in Data-Driven Decision-Making (DDM), Power Query, and quantitative modeling through Noroff.

Having served as a former student leader and human rights advocate in Zimbabwe, my research rejects superficial descriptions of political crises. Instead, I apply critical realism and systems-thinking to dissect how institutional structures, resource rents, and administrative coercion interact to constrain human freedom.

My flagship initiative, KUVHURA, formalizes this interdisciplinary approach into a live, mathematical observatory that proves citizen non-compliance is governed by material survival calculations rather than political apathy.
